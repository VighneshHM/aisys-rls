Every defect found while building and testing, with severity, steps to reproduce, owner, status, where it was fixed and how it was
retested. At the time of writing **no defect is open**.

**Legend.** Severity: **High** (data loss or corruption, security exposure, a scenario cannot be completed), **Medium** (wrong result
with a workaround, or a feature degraded), **Low** (wording, layout, convenience). Status: **Open** (found, not fixed), **Fixed**
(fixed and retested; the retest evidence column says how), **Won't fix** (none so far). **Owner** is the developer, VighneshHM,
working with the AI assistant. **Fixed in** gives the pull request, the branch and the version: every fix is in the `0.1.0` line (the
`version` in `pyproject.toml`) before the evaluation tag. Pull request numbers come from the merge history; #21 (`overall-fixes`)
is the number given by the project owner and was not yet merged when this was written. **Steps to reproduce** are the observable
trigger taken from each defect's description; they were not re-run for this log, they describe the original discovery.
